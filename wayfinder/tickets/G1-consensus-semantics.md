## Question

What does **"all groups agree"** mean mechanically before a manifesto can be published?

- Each group **approves its own** manifesto independently?
- **Cross-group consensus** across all groups (everyone signs off on a shared text)?
- A **facilitator sign-off**?
- A **vote / ack-count** threshold?

### Context
This is the **fuzziest product decision** and it gates publishing. It drives [Publish bridge](G2-publish-bridge.md) and graduates the G3 fog (publish lifecycle: revisions, retraction, versioning once public). Recommend a /grilling + /domain-modeling session here.

### What resolving this decides
The consensus model and the exact condition that unlocks publishing.

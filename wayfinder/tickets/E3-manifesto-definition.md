## Question

Define the **manifesto** — the LLM's templated output, which is a **policy recommendation for a more inclusive and fair community** derived from the two tables.

Specify: its **structure** (sections/headings), **audience**, **length**, **tone**, and the **template** the LLM fills (e.g., a preamble, the named actors/behaviors/failures surfaced, concrete policy/practice recommendations, commitments).

### Context
This is the "specialized and templated summary" from the Destination. Resolving it graduates the E4 fog (prompt assembly) and sets what [Manifesto editing](F1-manifesto-editing.md) works against.

### What resolving this decides
The canonical manifesto template/shape the system must produce.
